About the pins and the names. Each marker is a reference point, not the launch spot — usually the nearest car park, reserve entrance or road end, so it can sit 100–300 m from where you'll actually stand. Names are the official or most widely used name for the place; local signage, iwi names and some map apps differ, and a few spots go by more than one name. Navigate to the pin, then walk the last bit.

AvoidWeekends 09:00-16:00 (surfers); midday thermals bring paragliders

Best for: Black sand surf beach backed by 60 m cliffs and dunes - the most dramatic flying in Auckland, north or south.

Hazards: Active paraglider and hang-glider site (give way and stay well clear), strong onshore wind, surf and rips, soft sand kills motors.

Airspace is completely clean - 24 km from the nearest aerodrome. This is the best single spot in South Auckland by a distance. Pin is the main beach access off Karioitahi Road; the sand runs about 5 km south from here.

Before you fly

  • Log the flight on AirShare - required on council land, and it's how you request ATC or aerodrome approval.
  • Stay 30 m from people and dogs, 20 m from buildings, 50 m from playgrounds in use and infrastructure.
  • Nothing over 2.5 kg on council land without council permission.
  • FPV needs a spotter maintaining unaided line of sight - that's the CAA position, not optional.
  • Sept-March: stay 30 m off shorebirds on beaches, and don't take off within 100 m of them.
  • Inside a control zone or 4 km ring, the only recreational route is a Part 101 certificate plus approval - or a shielded operation.

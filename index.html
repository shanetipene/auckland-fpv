Rules and regulations

Every spot here assumes you are flying under Part 101. These are the three rule sets that decide where you can legally launch, and each one can override the others.

CAA Drone rules and pilot requirementsPart 101 and Part 102, pilot registration and competency, airspace classes, 120 m ceiling, and the FPV and night-flying conditions. This is the rule set everything else sits on top of. Auckland Council Council rules and guidelinesWhere you can and can't fly on council land, the regional park permit process, the 2.5 kg limit without council permission, and the code of conduct for flying over parks and reserves. DOC Department of Conservation rulesPublic conservation land needs a concession (permit) to take off, land or hover. Also the wildlife separation rules: 100 m to take off near wildlife, 50 m in flight, and 150 m from marine mammals.
